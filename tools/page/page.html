Check a bean bundle

A bundle is a folder of health records that one person chose to share. This page checks that every record is what that person signed and that nothing was added or removed. Then it shows what the bundle holds.

The check runs on this device. This page sends nothing: its script makes no request, and its policy blocks every load.
