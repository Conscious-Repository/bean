Check shared health records

Someone shared health records with you: one sealed file that opens with six words, or a folder. This page checks that every record is signed and that nothing was added or taken out. Then it shows the records.

The check runs on this device. This page sends nothing: its script makes no request, and its policy blocks every load.

Getting ready…

Signed by the identity with these words: This page cannot tell who that is. Ask the sender to read their identity words to you, by phone or face to face. If the words are not the same, do not rely on these records.
This file opened with the six words that you typed. The words keep the file closed on its way. They do not show who sealed it: anyone who knows them can seal another file under them.
